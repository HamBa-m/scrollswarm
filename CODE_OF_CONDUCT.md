# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free environment for everyone contributing to or using ScrollSwarm, regardless of age, background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected Standards

- **Respect & Empathy:** Treat contributors and community members with professional respect and patience.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Focus on objective, helpful feedback during code reviews and issue discussions.
- **Academic & Scientific Integrity:** Foster transparent, reproducible, and rigorous research practices.
- **Zero Tolerance:** Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior are strictly prohibited.
